What's new in SOBA

Release history and feature updates.

SOBA 1.2

August 2026

Core workflows

  • Drop — temporary shelf for files, folders, images, URLs, and more.
  • Later — bring something back at the right time.
  • Pin — keep references visible above your work.
  • Share — send selected items to trusted destinations.
  • Actions — contextual tools based on what SOBA detects.

Quick Capture

  • Open SOBA with ⌥ S.
  • Type, paste, or drop content directly.
  • Contextual handling for JSON, URLs, images, PDFs, addresses, timestamps, terminal commands, paths, and more.

Smart Actions

  • Image actions: preview, convert, resize, compress, metadata tools, OCR, and path actions.
  • PDF actions: Compress, Quick Look, Split, Extract Pages, and path utilities.
  • Folder/project actions: Terminal, IntelliJ, VS Code, Finder, path tools, and Compress.

Drag-to-edge

  • Configure SOBA to appear from the right, left, top, or bottom edge.
  • Drag files toward that edge to access Drop, Later, Pin, Share, and dynamic Quick Actions.

Polish & platform integration

  • Keyboard-only navigation across the shelf, Quick Capture, and Actions using arrow keys and Enter.
  • First-run guided tour covering Drop, Later, Pin, Share, Quick Capture, screen-edge activation, and contextual Actions.
  • Undo for reversible destructive actions such as clearing the shelf, removing pins, and dismissing Later items.
  • Optional iCloud sync for preferences and pins across a user's own Macs.
  • Focus Mode awareness to suppress resurfacing cards and click sounds while Focus is active.
  • Fuzzy search across shelf items for fast partial-name and imperfect-match lookup.

QR codes

  • Create a QR code directly from text or a URL.
  • Scan with any phone Camera app — no SOBA installation or pairing required.
